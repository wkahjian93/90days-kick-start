# 90Days Kick Start: how to use it

**The problem it solves:** the Trello board holds the syllabus, but you can't see at a glance which new agent is behind, which homework is missing, or when the next training needs booking.
**What the app does:** every agent gets their own 90-day board that starts on their join date. They submit homework as proof, and you approve it or send it back. You and the agent are both alerted when a week's training is due.

## For you (leader)

**Sign in** with your leader code (10+ characters, set once in Google).

**Team Progress screen**
- Four numbers at the top: active agents, average progress, items waiting for your review, overdue items.
- **Needs you today**, most urgent first:
  - 🟡 *Arrange training*: an agent's next week of training is due within 3 days, or already late. Book the session.
  - 🔵 *Review*: an agent submitted homework. Tap it to open it.
  - 🔴 *Overdue / Redo*: homework past its due date, or sent back and not yet fixed.
- **Agent cards** are sorted with the most at-risk first. Each shows day X of 90, % approved, items waiting review, overdue items and the next training.
  - *On track*: nothing overdue. *Behind*: 1–2 overdue. *At risk*: 3+ overdue, or anything more than 7 days late.

**Reviewing homework:** tap a card to see the notes, checklist and uploaded photos or files. Then choose:
- **✓ Approve**. On a training card the button says **✓ Training done**: tap it after you've run the session.
- **↺ Ask to redo**. You must write what to fix, and the agent sees your comment.
- **Reset**. This asks you to confirm first.

**Adding an agent:** tap **+ Add agent** and enter their name and start date (day 1). Email and phone are optional. The app gives an 8-letter sign-in code. Tap **Copy invite message** or **WhatsApp invite** to send it.

**If training is delayed:** open the agent and tap **Edit agent**, then move the start date. Every due date shifts, and the app asks you to confirm.

**Agent leaves:** use **Archive**. Their records are kept and they can't sign in. **Delete** is only for mistakes: you must type their name, and their files go to Google Drive trash, where they stay recoverable for 30 days.

**☰ Menu**
- **Email reminders** previews every email that would go out now. Nothing sends until you tap Send and confirm.
- **Settings** holds your name, your email, the app link, and the switch for **daily 8am emails**. Daily emails are off until you turn them on.
- **History** shows the last 60 actions: who submitted, approved or changed what.
- **Edit syllabus** lists every card by week. Tap a card to edit it, tap **＋ Add** to add one, and restore removed cards at the bottom.

## For the new agent

1. Open the link and enter your code. Then **add the app to your home screen**: on iPhone tap Share, then Add to Home Screen; on Android tap ⋮, then Install app.
2. **Your next steps** at the top tells you what's due, overdue or to redo.
3. Swipe the weeks left and right, or tap **W1, W2…** to jump. The coloured dot by each week means 🟢 all approved, 🔴 something late, 🔵 waiting for your leader, 🟡 due soon.
4. Tap a card to see the instructions and learning links. Tick the checklist, write your notes, and add photos or files (up to 8 MB each). Then tap **Submit for review**. **Save draft** keeps your work without submitting it.
5. **Activity A·O·P·F·C** is where you log approaches, appointments, presentations, follow-ups, closed cases and ANP each week. The app shows your O/A, P/O, F/P and closing ratios against the targets.

## How due dates work
- Day 1 is the start date. Week N is due on day 7×N, and Last Week and Goals on day 90.
- Setup: Register SG Member is due on day 3 and TCF on day 30 (**app defaults; Trello had no dates for these**). PEPY1 is due on day 180, from the Trello wording "within 6 months".
- The "arrange training" warning starts 3 days before a week's due date.

## Where the data lives
- Progress is kept in a private Google Sheet, **90Days Kick Start Data**, in your Google Drive.
- Homework files are kept in Drive under **90Days Kick Start Homework / agent name**.
- Only you, with the leader code, can see all agents. Each agent sees only their own board.

## Changing the syllabus
Only the leader can change cards. Use ☰ → **Edit syllabus**, the **＋ Add card** button at the bottom of any week on an agent's board, or **✎ Edit this card** inside a card. Each card can be for all agents or one agent only. Removing a card hides it but keeps any work already saved on it. Agents can only tick checklists, write notes and upload files.
